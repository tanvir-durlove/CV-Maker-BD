### Introduction
Welcome to **CV Maker - Resume builder** (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), owned and operated by **NextGen Tools** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the Application collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information gathered from users ("you" or "User").

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy. Because the Application is designed to build professional curriculum vitae (CVs) and resumes containing sensitive career and personal information, our core architecture ensures that **your CV content remains solely on your device**.

---

### 1. Data Handling & Resume Content Privacy

#### 1.1 Exclusively On-Device Data Storage
* **No Remote Storage or Account Creation:** The Application does not require account creation, logins, or cloud synchronization. All data you enter—including but not limited to your full name, contact information, photos, employment history, qualifications, skills, languages, and personal summaries—is stored exclusively in the private, sandboxed internal storage of your device.
* **No Server Inspection:** NextGen Tools does not transmit, copy, read, sell, share, or store your resume information on remote servers or third-party databases.
* **User Control and Deletion:** You retain complete control over your data. You may edit, duplicate, or delete individual documents at any time. Furthermore, the "Delete local data" option within Settings permanently purges all saved data and templates from your device.

#### 1.2 PDF Document Generation and Sharing
* The generation of PDF documents takes place locally on your device via the Android graphics and printing subsystem.
* Exported PDF files are created in your device's temporary cache directory and shared only when you explicitly invoke Android's standard share dialog (`ACTION_SEND`) or Print service (`PrintManager`).
* No documents are uploaded to our servers during document export or printing.

To provide access to professional templates, editing tools, and features without charging subscription fees, the Application utilizes third-party advertising services provided by **Google AdMob**.

#### 2.1 Information Collected by AdMob
Ad networks such as Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable technical information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. This may include:
* Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID).
* Device characteristics (manufacturer, model, operating system version, carrier).
* Approximate geographic location derived from IP address.
* Ad interaction events, impression data, and diagnostics for fraud prevention.

#### 2.2 Purpose of Ad Processing
This technical data is processed strictly to:
* Display relevant banner, rewarded, and interstitial advertisements.
* Prevent fraudulent click activity and enforce impression limits.
* Measure advertising performance and deliver rewards upon ad completion.

#### 2.3 Opting Out of Personalized Advertising
You may opt out of personalized advertisements or reset your Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings:
* Navigate to **Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID** (or **Reset advertising ID**).

For more details on how Google processes advertising data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms:  
[https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

### 3. Application Permissions

The Application adheres to the principle of least privilege and requests zero dangerous or invasive permissions:
* **No Storage Permissions Requested:** The Application does not request or require broad storage permissions (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, or `READ_MEDIA_*`). All file operations utilize secure Android `FileProvider` URIs (`content://`).
* **Internet Permission:** Used exclusively by advertising SDKs (Google AdMob) to deliver advertisements and verify test ad units.
